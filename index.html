SkepticsGPT Privacy Policy

Version 1.3 · Effective 18 October 2026

This policy explains what the SkepticsGPT Android app (package com.el.skepticsgpt) collects, why, who receives it, how long we keep it, and the choices you have. SkepticsGPT is an educational critical-thinking coach, not a fact-checking tool. It coaches the reasoning in arguments you choose to examine. It does not check facts or decide what is true.

Who we are

SkepticsGPT Devs develop and publish SkepticsGPT. In this policy, "we" and "us" mean SkepticsGPT Devs. Contact us at appropriate+frog-9dmyz3@norton.me with any privacy question or request.

Who is responsible for your data

Everything Lives LLC is the data controller for the personal information this policy describes, as the GDPR and the UK GDPR use that term. It decides why and how the app uses that information. To contact the controller about your data or your rights, email appropriate+frog-9dmyz3@norton.me.

What stays on your device

When you import a screenshot, the app reads its text on your phone with Google ML Kit. The image itself never leaves your device. Some features may use an on-device AI model; that processing also stays on your phone. Only text you then choose to analyze reaches our servers.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.
  • We do not use the text you analyze, your results, or anything we infer from them to choose ads.
  • We do not ask for, store, or guess your age.
  • We do not write the text you submit, your results, or your links into our server logs.
  • We do not scrape social media or read content from other apps.

AI processing

To analyze text, our servers on Google Cloud send it to OpenAI's API. We send requests with storage turned off, so OpenAI does not keep them for later retrieval, and under OpenAI's API terms it does not train its models on them. OpenAI may keep request data for a limited time to monitor abuse. Do not submit sensitive personal information, such as health, financial, or other people's private details. AI analyses can be wrong; each result shows its confidence and limits.

Ads

Ads appear only if you tap "Watch an ad" in the wallet. Google AdMob shows them. Before any ad, the app asks Google's User Messaging Platform whether ads are allowed and, where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland, and some US states), shows Google's consent message. We request non-personalized ads with a teen-appropriate content limit for everyone. AdMob may still collect device identifiers and usage data as described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. You can change or withdraw ad consent at any time through "Ad privacy choices" in the wallet.

App usage analytics

The app uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how people use the app and to improve it. Google processes this data for us as our service provider.

  • What it collects: the events Google Analytics records on its own: when the app is installed, opened, updated, or removed; sessions and time spent in the app; the screens you open; purchases and refunds through Google Play, with the product, its price, and the currency; and, if the app shows you an ad, that it appeared and any reward it paid. With these events it records the phone model, the Android and app versions, your language, a random app instance ID, and an approximate location, such as your country, region, or city, that Google works out from your network address.
  • What it never includes: the text you analyze, your results, the links you share, or your account. The app logs no events of its own and never gives Analytics your user ID.
  • No advertising use: the app stops Google Analytics from collecting the Android advertising ID and from using this data to personalize ads.
  • How long Google keeps it: up to 2 months after you last use the app, the Google Analytics default. Reports that only count users, without identifying anyone, may be kept longer.
  • How to turn it off: in the app, open Profile, go to Privacy & transparency, and turn off Share app usage analytics. It is on until you turn it off, so the app collects usage analytics from its first launch, including before you sign in and can reach Profile. The choice belongs to your phone, not your account, so signing out keeps it. Turning it off stops collection at once and clears the usage data your phone has not sent yet. Data already sent stays with Google Analytics until the period above ends.

Crash reports

If the app crashes or freezes, it can send a crash report to Firebase Crashlytics, so we can find and fix the problem. Google processes this data for us as our service provider.

  • What a report holds: where in the app's code it crashed or froze (stack traces and the names of the errors), the phone model, the Android and app versions, free memory and disk space, and random installation IDs from Crashlytics and Firebase. If usage analytics is on, a report also holds recent usage events, such as which screen was open.
  • What it never includes: your text, your messages, your results, or your account. The app removes every error message before a report is made, because an error message can quote what you typed, and it adds no user ID, notes, or custom data of its own.
  • How long it is kept: Crashlytics keeps reports for 90 days.
  • How to turn it off: in the app, open Profile, go to Privacy & transparency, and turn off Send crash reports. Crash reports are on by default. Turning them off also deletes any report your phone has not sent.

Who receives your information

  • Google, as our service provider for Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Functions, App Check, Analytics, Crashlytics), Google Play billing and Play Integrity, and AdMob.
  • OpenAI, which processes text you submit for analysis.
  • Meta, only when you choose to sign in with Facebook. Meta then receives what it needs to sign you in, such as your Facebook login and basic device details, under Facebook's own terms. At other times the app sends Meta nothing. It turns off the automatic app events in Facebook's software, and it never sends Meta your advertising ID.
  • Authorities, if the law requires it, or to protect the rights and safety of users and the service.

Our providers process data in the United States and other countries. Where the law requires, they use approved safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

How long we keep it

  • Saved analyses: 30 days, or until you delete them.
  • Pending analysis records: deleted about 30 days after they finish.
  • Account, profile, token, purchase, and ad-reward records: while your account exists. After you delete your account, we keep financial records only as long as tax, accounting, or fraud-prevention law requires.
  • App usage analytics: up to 2 months after you last use the app, the Google Analytics default.
  • Crash reports: 90 days.

Your choices and rights

  • Saved history is off until you turn it on in your profile, and you can turn it off at any time.
  • Ad consent can be changed through "Ad privacy choices".
  • Usage analytics is on until you turn off "Share app usage analytics" in Profile, under Privacy & transparency.
  • Crash reports are on until you turn off "Send crash reports" in the same place.
  • Access, correction, export, and deletion. You can edit your display name in the app. To get a copy of your data or delete your account and its data, email us from the address on your account. We answer within 30 days. Uninstalling the app does not delete your account.
  • Depending on where you live, including the EEA, the UK, and US states such as California, you may also have the right to object to or restrict processing, to withdraw consent, and to complain to your data protection authority. We do not treat you differently for using these rights.

Our legal bases, where the GDPR applies: performing our contract with you (account, analyses, tokens, purchases), your consent (saved history and ads), and our legitimate interests (security, abuse prevention, and improving the app, including usage analytics and crash reports, which you can turn off at any time).

Children

SkepticsGPT is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

We encrypt data in transit, deny apps direct access to financial records, check every token change on our servers, and give access only to people who need it. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot promise absolute security.

Changes to this policy

When we change this policy we update its version and effective date on this page. For a significant change we will also tell you in the app.
